The model uses displacement and BMEP to set torque, then derives horsepower from torque and RPM.
Core relationship (4-stroke piston engines): Torque (lb-ft) ≈ BMEP (psi) × displacement (in³) ÷ 150.8. Horsepower = torque (lb-ft) × RPM ÷ 5,252. Layout profiles are illustrative curve assumptions, not universal rules for every engine.
